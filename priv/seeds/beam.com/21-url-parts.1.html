A build URL of nifs+= whose NIFs are in the parts of the release program-parts: the edge beam.com and the NIF objects of vix, rusty_csv and ex_pdfium, and the NIF files of tailwind.com (scripts/file_nifs.sh), as the job edge-parts of build.yml cuts them. No build of the URL is in the release. The names go into the name of a list: vix-* is vix (the object has each part of vix), and the default NIFs go away, because beam.com has them. The answer is the segments of the list, one after the other, at once and with no build. It has no Content-Length (section 8.7 of the specification). With a different NIF, such as picosat_elixir, there is no list, and 12-build builds the file.
